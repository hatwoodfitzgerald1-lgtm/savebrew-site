# Home (/) measures, B1

Measured with Playwright (Chromium 1194 headless) against the B1 dev server at 1440 by 900, September 24, 2026, per finalshot/references/measure_budgets.md. The scratch scripts: measure-home.mjs (LCP, CLS, scroll length, fps), framecost.mjs (the loom's per frame cost), the JS weight from the production build (`npm run build`, dist/client, gzip level 9).

| Budget | Measured | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| LCP under 2.5s (loader active) | 432 to 624ms across five runs, the LCP element is the H1's first line (`span.hm-hero__h1-line`) | pass |
| CLS under 0.1 | 0.000 (poster path), 0.033 to 0.037 (loom path; the cross dissolve of the canvas over the poster and the hero knots' draw in) | pass |
| Initial JS under 350KB gzipped, excluding the lazy Three.js bundle | 20.1KB (the site's own modules loaded at first paint: Layout 5.7, Home 3.2, Ranker 2.3, Header 1.3, cart 1.3, CartDrawer 1.1, product-ui 0.9, preload-helper 0.8, ease 0.6, ProductView 0.1, plus 2.7 of inline scripts) plus GSAP 3.12.5 core, ScrollTrigger and Flip from cdnjs 55.3KB, deferred after first paint: 75.3KB in all. Three.js r128 plus the vendored Line2 classes, 154.4KB gzipped, are fetched only after first paint on desktop with a fine pointer and are excluded per the budget. The loom module itself is a lazy chunk (14.5KB raw). | pass |
| 55fps or better through the 1.3vh signature scroll | Poster and DOM docking path (no WebGL, `--disable-gpu`): 60fps average over 4s of programmatic scrolling, 240 frames, worst frame gap 33ms, p95 17ms. WebGL path: the sandbox has no GPU; with SwiftShader software GL every composited frame of the whole page costs 70 to 500ms (14fps even with Three.js blocked), so no fps figure for the live loom can be read here. The loom's own per frame cost on the main thread (scene update, five warp sags, the weft, the DOM docking, `renderer.render`) is 0.7 to 2.9ms at progress 0, 0.3, 0.55, 0.75 and 0.95 (66 draw calls, 20.9k triangles, one WebGL context, dpr 1.0 in the sandbox, capped at 1.5). The low power fallback was exercised by the sandbox itself: under 30fps after two seconds of sampling the scene drops to the poster and the docking keeps running on the static table, which is what the measurement above then recorded at 60fps. To be re-measured on the live webflow.io URL on a machine with a GPU during post deploy QA. | pass on the DOM path; the WebGL path needs the live measurement |
| Signature scroll length 1.25 to 1.75 viewport heights | `?qa=arc` logs "THE PASS timeline: start 0px, end 1170px, length 1.30 viewport heights" at 900 tall (the ScrollTrigger runs 0 to 1.3vh, `invalidateOnRefresh`, not pinned) | pass |

Other numbers observed:
- The loader (the selvedge stitch) ties off at `sb:ready` once fonts, the DOM and the Three.js fetch settle; on the mobile path it tied off with `data-loaded="true"` within 2.5s of navigation in the sandbox. With SwiftShader the loom path starves requestAnimationFrame, so the stitch's 4s cap is what finishes it there; on hardware the fetch of three.min.js (about 150KB gzipped) sets the tie off, inside the 2s target on broadband.
- Text measures (average characters per line of running text, measured in the browser): five column cells 24 to 30 at 1280 and 1440 (the archetype's own minimum: about 15px at 1280 and 16px at 1440 inside the row boards, per Art Direction 0.2), 29 to 36 at 1920, 38 to 54 at 2560; the membership inclusions 48 at 1280 and 1440 and 29 to 41 at 1920 and 2560 (two columns from 1700px); the standfirst 48 at 1440, 30 at 1920, 40 at 2560; the heading pass intros 50 at 1440, 38 at 1920, 50 at 2560.
- Poster: /assets/scene/loom-poster.webp, 1600 by 1000, 195KB, rendered from the scene at progress 1.0 with the 4 percent grain baked in (registered as the slot `scene.loom-poster`).
